Executive Abstract

As the multi-trillion-dollar intergenerational wealth transfer accelerates across North America, institutional advancement offices confront unprecedented ethical and strategic transformations. This research explores how gift officers and philanthropy directors balance fiduciary obligations, donor intent protection, and family governance while maintaining ethical integrity in planned gift solicitations.

Through mixed-method qualitative interviews with advancement directors and quantitative audits of planned gift retention across performing arts and healthcare foundations, this study establishes an empirical model for values-aligned moves management that bridges donor aspirations with permanent community impact.

Table of Contents (Working Outline)

  • Chapter I: Foundations of Philanthropic Ethics — Historical evolution of donor intent from common law to modern endowment agreements.
  • Chapter II: The Legal & Probate Landscape — Operational case studies in bequest enforcement, probate litigation, and testamentary trust compliance.
  • Chapter III: Moves Management in the Next Generation — Adapting cultivation cycles for millennial and Gen Z benefactors focused on transparency.
  • Chapter IV: Model Stewardship Matrix — A proposed evidence-based framework for values-aligned planned giving programs.
