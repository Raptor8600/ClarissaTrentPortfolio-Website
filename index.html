A passionate advocate for thoughtful institutional advancement, ethical fundraising, and cultural stewardship.

Indiana University • Lilly Family School of Philanthropy

Ethical Frameworks in Intergenerational Wealth Transfer: Reimagining Moves Management & Planned Giving Stewardship

Candidate: Clarissa Trent (they/them) • Degree: Master of Arts in Philanthropic Studies
Research Abstract

As the multi-trillion-dollar intergenerational wealth transfer accelerates, institutional advancement offices confront unprecedented ethical and operational transformations. This research explores how gift officers and philanthropy leaders balance fiduciary obligations, donor intent protection, and family governance while maintaining ethical integrity in planned gift solicitations.

Core Chapters & Theoretical Inquiries
  • Chapter I: Foundations of Philanthropic Ethics — Historical evolution of donor intent from common law to modern endowment bylaws.
  • Chapter II: The Legal & Probate Landscape — Operational case studies in bequest enforcement, probate litigation, and testamentary trust administration.
  • Chapter III: Moves Management for Next-Gen Benefactors — Adapting cultivation cycles for millennial and Gen Z benefactors focused on trust and transparency.
  • Chapter IV: Model Stewardship Matrix — An empirical framework for values-aligned planned giving programs across higher education and healthcare.
